# VISIE — Sengoku Soldier (owner vision, Oct 9–10)

**Status:** 📝 **decided by the owner, to be confirmed after the playtest.** Points marked **⚑ canon change** go into the World Bible only after the owner has played the new build.
Made with the owner, step by step, from his old ChatGPT stories (ARC 1 + ARC 2: 852 turns), the ARC 1 manhwa, the 1540 plan and the other anime RPG chats (copies in `docs/sources/`). It was then checked against the canon (24 critique points) and fixed. Where this file and the canon disagree and no ⚑ is marked, **the canon wins.**

## 1. The core
- **Core sentence:** *"One boy, seven kingdoms at war, and a father's name he must not say. Will he make it?"*
- **#1 feeling:** the **underdog rise**, like Shin in *Kingdom*. Li starts as nothing and claws one step higher every session. Nobody gives him anything.
- **Inspiration:** *Kingdom* (slave to great general, huge battles, generals with aura, realistic ranks) and *Black Clover / Naruto* (hidden power, rivals, the underdog shouting his dream).
- **Player role: co-author.** The player picks options or writes anything. Text in **(brackets)** is a **wish to the Game Master for the story**: more drama, a harder duel, a flashback, a rival should appear. Claude honours wishes in how the story is told. **Wishes never change numbers, powers, abilities, facts or rules.** If a wish asks for that, Claude says so in one short out-of-character line.
- **Tone:** like *Kingdom*. Brutal war: friends die, sacrifices happen, betrayal is real, but hope and hype win through. Li never dies (hidden rule, never shown).
- **Powers:** no magic (canon). Aura, the Zone, clan techniques and ability ranks are all superhuman skill.
- **Deaths:** allies, mentors and friends can die for good. It hurts, and it's remembered forever.
- **Language:** English.

## 2. The story
- **World:** the seven kingdoms (canon). The Empire fell in the Burning (Year 0); a hundred years of war followed.
- **The secret:** Li **half-knows** (canon 10.2). He has fragments only (two swords on a wall, a laugh, "Little Dragon", "Never say that name"). **The player learns the truth together with Li.**
- **Opening:**
  1. **Film:** the Empire → the Burning → seven kingdoms → a hundred years of war → **the legend of the Twin Demon as the world tells it**, including the official lie ("killed by Kurogane raiders"). The film never says Li is his son and never shows the real killers.
  2. **Playable childhood:** the **snowstorm** (Year 95, Month 8, canon). Li (7) is on the flight north behind the man with the white-rock crest. His mother says: "Never say the name Hattori." He goes back for a dropped bundle, falls, and is found by slavers. He only says "Li". The choices colour the moment and the first journal lines, never the outcome.
  3. **"Five years later":** Year 100, Li is 12, a slave of the Red Crane band.
- **Structure: a free world with long goals** (not promises, not a script):
  - **Life stages** (slave → soldier → officer → general → legend) only describe where Li stands. The route between them is free.
  - **Five long goals:** (1) Breaking the chains, (2) Blood and the Zone, (3) The name (the world hears "Hattori" from his mouth), (4) Home (mother and sisters), (5) His father's death (the truth and the people behind it).
  - **Claude steers so that a *chance* toward the next open goal keeps coming.** The player can miss or lose a chance; a missed chance returns later in another form. Nothing is guaranteed and nothing is forced.
  - **Early family lead:** during the slave years, the world plants a *lead* that the family may be alive (a rumour, a crest, a traveller). It's never the full truth or their location.
  - **The living world clock** (canon fuses, raids, kings, wars) runs whether Li is there or not.
  - **Allegiance is free:** any kingdom, switching sides, or mercenary work. The world reacts realistically.
- **Endgame:** become the best general ever. The story can go on after that.
- **Time skips: chained skips.** The player can ask "skip to when I'm 14", "until the next battle" or "until something important", and gives a plan. The game runs scene by scene within the canon cap (a slave skips at most 1 month per step) and **stops at anything important**. Results are the same as playing it (canon ch. 9). A recap follows every skip.

## 3. One turn
- **Shape:**
  1. a header (year, title, place, season, Li's age)
  2. the scene
  3. progress (see 4)
  4. 3–4 options, which can be combined ("1 and 3"), plus free text and (wishes)
  5. small buttons
- **Length** (canon plus a bit): small moment ~100 words · normal ~200 · big moment ~300. When a big scene needs more, it continues in a "Continue" beat.
- **Two speeds:** normal scenes, and the combat system. Before an important fight (an officer, a rival, a key moment) Claude asks: "Fight it yourself, or simulate?" Small fights are simulated.
- **Duel display:**
  - wound and fatigue tracks for both fighters
  - **the Zone counted in rounds** (it starts at 1–2 rounds and grows with mastery; 3 uses per battle at first): "Zone: 2 rounds left · 1 of 3 uses"
  - the enemy's style, with hidden abilities shown as "???"
  - 3 moves, or write your own

  There are no visible dice, and duels must be hard.
- **Style:** normal scenes are clean prose. Big moments get cinematic hype: short lines, the name in big letters, a legend card.
- **Symbols ⚑ canon change:** plain marks by default (▲ ▼ and coloured words: NEW ALLY · TROPHY · WOUNDED). **A few emoji are allowed on chips.** (The canon said none.)
- **Surprises happen often:** flashbacks, side dialogues, rivals showing up, soldiers with requests.
- **Speed and cost: a smart mix.** The strongest model writes the big moments and fight aftermaths; a faster tier writes normal and skip scenes. Text streams in, so the player reads while it writes.

## 4. Li growing
- **Numbers stay as approved** (Numbers Sheet v0.3; canon start VAL 14 at 12).
- **Age targets** (VAL under 25–30 at 15, ~45–50 at 17, great generals 80–95) get **tested with the career simulation first**, then tuned (`docs/reports/career-pace-*.md`).
- **Growth must feel rewarding without fake numbers.** Every turn shows **progress bars** ("VAL 14 → 15: 60%", mastery, skills, bonds), so something visibly moves; a whole point becomes a real moment.
- Growth comes from training and mentors (small, capped per season), fights the player plays himself (much more), near-death moments (abilities), and books and clan secrets.
- **Mastery /100** per style and per ability; unlocks at 30 / 50 / 70 / 100 (canon).
- **Two kinds of rank (canon):** power rank F–S from stats, and command rank from merit *and* birth (a Japanese title plus the number of men). A former slave commands fewer men than an equally strong noble.
- **The Zone (SHINKEN NO RYŪ)** wakes only at a real near-death moment. It's limited (rounds, uses), mastery grows slowly, and Li collapses afterwards.
- **Aura** acts on morale and the opponent's choices only (canon).
- **Everyone grows** (other people age, train, rise, get wounded): the next build round, with NPC tiers (canon ch. 8).
- **Domain kept light:** family or officers run the land; Li gives big orders.
- **Tracked:** loyalty per ally (shown as a summary), unit morale, fame and titles, and **trophies** (name, rank and men commanded, battle, date).

## 5. The screen
- **Mostly laptop**, phone second. Keep the approved ink war scroll, but **more manhwa**: title cards, silhouettes, motion in big moments.
- **The dashboard is always visible:** a side panel on laptop, a slim tappable bar on the phone.
- **Pages:**
  - **Story**
  - **Map**, with two views:
    - **"Li's eyes"** (default): only what Li knows.
    - **"The world":** only **public** facts (kingdoms, borders, capitals, public wars, famous battles and generals). It never shows hidden plots, fuses, secret armies or hidden values (canon rule).
  - **Li:** sheet, trophies, unit or army, domain
  - **People**
  - **Journal + Recap**
  - **Encyclopedia**
- **Buttons, not typed commands:** Recap, Li, World, Skip ahead, Something's wrong, Download save.

## 6. The memory
- **The golden rule:** the code keeps the facts, not Claude's head. Claude proposes changes; the code checks and stores them and hands the true facts back every turn.
- **A world simulation, not only Li.** The world keeps moving.
- **Never forget:** Li's sheet, every person (name, role, rank, bond, promises, alive or dead, where), every event and trophy with its date, secrets (who knows what), open threads, projects, the world clock.
- **Chapter recaps:** every ~20 scenes the code stores a chapter recap (written once, never rewritten). Claude gets all chapter recaps, the last 20 scenes, and every person and fact.
- **Automatic fact-check (errors are prevented, not only reported):** before a scene is shown, the code checks it against the memory (names spelled right, dead people silent, Li's age, rank and status right, no game words in the story). If something is off, Claude rewrites it once.
- **"Something's wrong" button:** the player describes the error, Claude proposes corrections, the player confirms, and it's stored as an event.
- **Ironman stays:** a fair loss or a bad choice is never undone. Going back exists **only for real bugs** (max 3 scenes), and every rewind is logged.
- **Saving, next build round:**
  1. the account database (a shared, owner-only path that Claude can read to fix bugs), after checking the storage limits
  2. an add-only event log with snapshots
  3. a local browser copy
  4. a "Download save" file with a version number

## Phase 2 decisions (open since Oct 6) — 📝 decided by Claude at the owner's request, owner can overrule
1. **Escape** stays possible but gets harder: only in **winter quarters** (Months 10–12), where the stealth and endurance bar still applies. Price unchanged (hunted, people left behind).
2. **Buying freedom** gets real ways to earn: Sukezō's medicines sold to the levy, dice at the fire on paydays (deterministic, by observation skill), and carrying for the Hagiwara villages. Target: a hard-working buyer is free around 15.
3. **Officers** come when battles offer them (canon Numbers §6), not on two fixed days a year. In Game Master mode, Claude places them in raids and skirmishes, and the referee only allows officer heads through a won fight.
4. **A runaway** gets his own start in Tatara (smithy or militia, hunters on his trail), designed with Stage 2.
5. **Raizen's share** stays as built (respect > 50; half of 0.1 ryō per merit).
