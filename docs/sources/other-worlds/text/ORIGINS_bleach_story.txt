
ORIGINS – The Beginning of Brom Hakamara 🌑
Race (Start): Human
Personality: Confident, strong-willed, a show-off, and a natural fighter.
Life Before Death: A normal life, popular at school and work, but always craved something more exciting.
Death: Died saving a young girl from being hit by a car.

👁️ AWAKENING AS A HOLLOW 👁️
Became a standard Hollow after death, consumed by instinct and hunger.
Started hunting other Hollows instead of humans, slowly becoming stronger.
Developed a love for combat and dominance.

💀 EVOLUTION – From Hollow to Gillian to Adjuchas 💀
Reached the Menos Forest and engaged in brutal battles for survival.
Became a Gillian (Menos Grande) but refused to stay mindless.
Ate countless other Menos, forcing an evolution into an Adjuchas.
Gained a distinct form with a muscular, humanoid body and clawed arms.

🔥 ASCENSION – Becoming a Vasto Lorde 🔥
Kept evolving, refusing to stop consuming stronger Hollows.
Eventually became a Vasto Lorde, one of the rarest and most powerful Hollow forms.
Developed a black and red armored exoskeleton, powerful claws, and incredible speed & strength.
Clashed with other Vasto Lordes to prove dominance.

⚔️ THE LAS NOCHES INVASION – Challenging the Espada ⚔️
Stormed Las Noches, arrogant and eager to prove himself.
Fought Nnoitra (Espada #5) and pushed him to his limits, proving he was almost Espada-level even as a Vasto Lorde.
Was ultimately defeated and captured, taken to Aizen.

👑 REBIRTH – Becoming an Arrancar 👑
Stood before Aizen and chose to remove his mask, embracing his Arrancar transformation.
New Name: 🔥 Kaizar Drega 🔥
Fighting Style: A mix of brutal hand-to-hand combat and deadly clawed gauntlets on his right arm.
Current Rank: Unassigned, must prove his strength to be placed in the Espada.
Current Battle: Fighting Grimmjow to test his Arrancar abilities.

🛡️ SUMMARY OF KAIZAR DREGA’S ABILITIES 🛡️
Fighting Style: Brutal Brawler – A mix of raw strength, clawed attacks, and hand-to-hand mastery.
Primary Weapon: A heavy gauntlet-claw on his right arm for devastating slashes and grappling moves.
Cero Proficiency: Strong but prefers close combat. Can use Cero Oscuras when in Resurrección.
Resurrección: 🔥 (Not yet revealed – Will be unlocked soon!) 🔥
Speed & Strength: On par with the upper-tier Espada, but still learning how to fully use his Arrancar power.
