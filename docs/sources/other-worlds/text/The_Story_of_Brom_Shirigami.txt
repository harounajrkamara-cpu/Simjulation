
The Story of Brom Shirigami – From the Beginning Until Now!

🌑 Rebirth – A New Life in the Naruto World!
You were reincarnated into the world of Naruto as Brom Shirigami, a young shinobi with no memory of your past life. You woke up in Konohagakure (Hidden Leaf Village), realizing that you had been given a second chance at life—but also that you weren’t just any ordinary ninja.
✅ Your Origins:
You were born into the Shirigami Clan, a mysterious and erased bloodline with a Cursed Kekkei Genkai.
Your past village was wiped out by the Great Nations due to your clan’s dangerous abilities.
You had no family left, but you were raised as a Leaf Genin.

🔥 The Chunin Exams – Your First True Challenge!
You participated in the Chunin Exams, proving yourself as a capable fighter.
💀 Forest of Death:
You played it smart, avoiding fights early and training instead of engaging in risky battles.
Eventually, you made it to the tower without much trouble, securing your scrolls.
⚔️ 1v1 Battles:
First Match: You fought a Puppet User! You used your Wind Release to disrupt his puppets and went hand-to-hand, crushing him.
Final Match: You fought Shikamaru Nara. You knew you were outmatched, so you fought smart but ultimately chose to surrender—just like he did in the anime.
✅ Your performance was strong, and even though you didn’t win, you showed promise.

📚 The Truth About Your Bloodline – The Shirigami Clan’s Dark Past
After the exams, you began training seriously. You asked Aoba Yamashiro, an experienced Jonin, to be your mentor.
💀 Discovering Your Kekkei Genkai:
You learned from the Leaf Archives that your Shirigami Bloodline was wiped out due to its dangerous powers.
Your Kekkei Genkai allowed fast healing (at the cost of pain) and the ability to drain chakra from enemies.
The village classified your clan as too dangerous—if they found out you were a survivor, they might try to control or eliminate you.
You chose to keep it a secret and only told Aoba.

⚡ Training Arc – Wind & Lightning Mastery!
🔥 You focused on combining Wind and Lightning Release into a deadly fighting style.
✅ Techniques You Developed:
Lightning Dash – Increases speed unpredictably.
Wind Slash – A ranged attack that can be infused with Lightning to stun enemies.
Storm Fang – A dashing Wind-boosted Lightning strike for high damage.
🩸 Your Bloodline Training:
You experimented with your Cursed Bloodline and realized it could awaken more powers.
However, pushing it too far made your body feel unstable… something is still dormant inside you.

👥 Your New Team – The Squad Assembles!
With Aoba’s help, you joined a new squad with two other Genin:
🔥 Reika Inuzuka – A wild fighter from the Inuzuka Clan, fights alongside her ninja wolf Kibaeru.
⚡ Tetsuo Kamizuru – A strategic fighter from the Kamizuru Clan, controls bees for offense and Genjutsu.
✅ Your team had strong potential, but you needed to build trust.
