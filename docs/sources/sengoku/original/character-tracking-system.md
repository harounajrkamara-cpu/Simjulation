## 📚 CHARACTER TRACKING COMPONENTEN

### BASIC INFO TRACKING:
- **Name**: Full character name
- **Age**: Current age + birthday tracking
- **Clan**: Which clan they belong to
- **Stam**: Which stam within the clan  
- **Position**: Current rank/role + promotion history
- **Location**: Current location + movement patterns

### STATS & ABILITIES TRACKING:
- **LEA/VAL/INT/POL**: Current levels + history of all changes
- **Rank**: Current power rank (F-S) + rank progression
- **Skills**: All mastery levels (0-100) + training progress
- **Abilities**: Special abilities + mastery levels + unlock conditions
- **Equipment**: Current weapons, armor, tools + upgrades
- **Growth Events**: Exactly what caused stat increases/decreases

### RELATIONSHIPS DYNAMICS:
- **Loyalty To**: Who they're loyal to + exact loyalty percentages
- **Rivals**: Who they compete with + rivalry intensity levels
- **Friends**: Close relationships + friendship strength
- **Romantic**: Any romantic interests, marriages, attractions
- **Family**: Family connections, obligations, inherited loyalties

### PERSONALITY & PSYCHOLOGY:
- **Core Traits**: Main personality characteristics
- **Motivations**: What drives their decisions
- **Fears**: What they're afraid of or avoid
- **Goals**: Short-term and long-term objectives
- **Secrets**: Hidden information, past events, conspiracies

### MEMORY & HISTORY:
- **Key Events**: Major events they experienced + their perspective
- **Battles**: All battles participated in + their performance
- **Decisions**: Important choices they made + consequences
- **Player Interactions**: EVERY conversation with you + their memory of it
- **Reputation**: How they view you + how others view them

### CURRENT STATUS:
- **Health**: Physical condition, injuries, recovery status
- **Mental State**: Confidence level, stress, morale, emotional state
- **Resources**: Money, equipment quality, troops commanded
- **Active Plots**: What they're currently planning/scheming
- **Availability**: Where they are + daily schedule + accessibility
