# Sources — the owner's old stories and documents

Copied Oct 10 from the owner's PC (`Documents/Simulatei Chatgpt`) at his request. The folders are **per world**, because the game will have more worlds later.
`original/` holds the files as they were (.docx, .pdf, .md, .py, .html). `text/` holds plain-text copies of the .docx files, for searching and for Claude.

⚠️ **These are history, not canon.** They use older versions of the story: "Ali" the slave in real Japan in 1543, and Li at 16 in Shiina territory in 1540. The World Bible wins over everything here (CLAUDE.md canon order).
